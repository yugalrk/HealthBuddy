# Privacy Policy — HealthBuddy

**Last updated: 12 September 2026**

## The short version

HealthBuddy does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data
anywhere except on your own device.

## What the app stores

To build your meal plan, HealthBuddy saves the following **on your phone only**:

- The household details you enter: age, sex, height, weight, activity level and
  goal for each person.
- Your dietary preference, food likes and dislikes, and any allergies you mark.
- The current week's meal plan and which shopping items you have ticked off.

This information is written to your device's local app storage. It is never
uploaded, never sent to us, and never shared with anyone.

## What the app does NOT do

- It does **not** require an account or a sign-in.
- It does **not** contain analytics, tracking, or advertising libraries.
- It does **not** request internet access. The app has no `INTERNET` permission
  in its manifest, so it is technically incapable of sending your data anywhere.
- It does **not** collect location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers.

## Deleting your data

Everything is removed when you either:

- use **Household → Reset everything** inside the app, or
- uninstall the app.

Because nothing is stored off your device, there is no server-side copy to
request the deletion of.

## Children

The app allows you to add children as members of your household so that meals
and shopping quantities are sized correctly. This information stays on your
device like everything else. The app is not directed at children as users.

## Health disclaimer

HealthBuddy provides general nutrition guidance based on the ICMR-NIN 2020
*Nutrient Requirements for Indians* and published food composition data. It is
**not medical advice** and is not a substitute for a doctor or a registered
dietitian. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or managing a condition such as
diabetes, kidney disease, a thyroid disorder or an eating disorder, follow the
advice of your healthcare professional.
