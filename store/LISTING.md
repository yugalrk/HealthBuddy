# Play Store listing copy

## App name (30 char max)
HealthBuddy: Weekly Meal Plan

## Short description (80 char max)
Weekly Indian meal plans and one shopping list, balanced for your whole family.

## Full description (4000 char max)

Plan a whole week of Indian meals in seconds — and get the one shopping list
that covers it.

HealthBuddy asks about everyone who eats at home, what you like, and what you
are aiming for. Then it builds seven days of breakfasts, lunches, snacks and
dinners, sized for your household, with a single categorised shopping list to
match.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

• Balanced across the week, not just the meal
Most planners treat each meal separately. HealthBuddy carries a shortfall
forward — a lighter lunch is made up at dinner, a short Tuesday is repaid on
Wednesday. What matters is the weekly total, and that is what it optimises.

• Takes vegetarian protein seriously
ICMR-NIN recommends a higher protein intake for cereal-based Indian diets
because the protein quality is lower. HealthBuddy applies that adjustment
automatically and plans dals, paneer, tofu, soya and sprouts to actually reach
it. This is the problem the app was built to solve.

• Sized for your household
Two adults, a teenager and a grandparent all need different amounts. Tell the
app who eats at home and every recipe quantity and shopping figure scales to
suit.

• A shopping list you can actually use
Grouped by aisle, rounded up to real pack sizes, and it does not tell you to buy
turmeric every single week. Mark what is already in your kitchen and it drops
straight off the list.

• Iron and calcium too
The two nutrients Indian diets most often fall short on are planned for
explicitly, not left to chance.

BUILT ON PUBLISHED SCIENCE

Energy needs use the Mifflin-St Jeor equation. Nutrient targets follow ICMR-NIN
2020 (Nutrient Requirements for Indians). Food values come from the Indian Food
Composition Tables 2017 and USDA FoodData Central, and the app tells you which
source each number came from.

COMPLETELY PRIVATE

No account. No sign-in. No ads. No trackers. The app does not even request
internet permission — your household's details never leave your phone.

VEGETARIAN, EGGETARIAN OR NON-VEGETARIAN

Set your preference once and only matching meals are ever planned. Mark a true
allergy and that ingredient is excluded absolutely.

---

HealthBuddy offers general nutrition guidance and is not medical advice. If you
are pregnant or managing a health condition, please follow your doctor or
dietitian.
